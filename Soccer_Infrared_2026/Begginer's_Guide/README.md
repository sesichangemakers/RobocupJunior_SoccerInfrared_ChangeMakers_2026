<h1>Beginner's Guide</h1>

<p>Directory dedicated to introductory materials developed by the Soccer Infrared team to help new members and teams take their first steps in the competition.</p>

<h2>Contents</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Fundamental concepts for understanding the competition and its requirements.</li>
  <li>Practical guidelines for teams that are new to Soccer Infrared.</li>
  <li>A guide authored by our team to help new teams get started with the modality.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Purpose</h2>
<p>To provide a clear and organized foundation for new members and teams, helping them understand the fundamentals of Soccer Infrared and take their first steps in the modality.</p>

<p>This material was created by our team to share the knowledge and experience we have gained throughout our development, providing a practical starting point for teams entering the competition.</p>
