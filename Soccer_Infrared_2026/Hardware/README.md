<h1>Hardware</h1>

<p>Directory dedicated to the mechanical designs, electronic circuits, and manufacturing files developed by the SESI ChangeMakers Soccer Infrared team for the 2026 season.</p>
<h2>Contents</h2>
<ul>
	<li><a href="3D_Models/">3D Models:</a> three-dimensional models and robot assembly files, including the version 4 mechanical assembly.</li>
	<li><a href="Electronics/">Electronics:</a> electronic diagrams, board schematics, and Gerber files for the robot's upper, intermediate, lower, and kicker circuit boards.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Purpose</h2>
<p>To organize and share the project's hardware resources, facilitating assembly, maintenance, and future development.</p>
